# Industries and materials expansion

Eight application pages: automotive components, industrial automation, electronics/optics, energy/charging/storage equipment, molds/tooling/fixtures, security/access equipment, fluid control, and general machinery. These pages describe application discussions and RFQ requirements. They do not invent certifications, approved customer lists or project histories. Existing automotive supply-chain experience remains as confirmed by the user.

Seven material detail pages distinguish carbon/alloy steel, 10B21, SUS304/316, aluminum, copper, brass and titanium. Strength examples include the grade/condition, unit, source and whether the figure is nominal, a specification minimum or a typical stock reference. Finished fastener class values and stock material properties are not treated as equivalent load ratings.

Key source checks:
- Steel classes: nominal class designation references (800/640, 1000/900, 1200/1080 MPa) are explicitly not presented as the ISO acceptance minimum table. Size and product scope remain applicable.
- Austenitic fastener classes 70 and 80: minimum tensile 700/800 MPa and 0.2% stress 450/600 MPa, subject to the applicable ISO 3506 scope. A raw SUS304/316 grade does not itself establish the class.
- Hydro 6061-T6/T6511 extruded reference: 260/240 MPa; 6082-T6/T6511 listed 5–150 mm range: 310/260 MPa. Other product forms or dimensions require their applicable specifications.
- CDA C11000 H02 stock tensile reference approximately 290 MPa. C36000 H02 rod typical 386/310 MPa. Copper and brass grade/temper distinctions are maintained; C36000 composition limitations are noted.
- Carpenter Ti-6Al-4V annealed minimum specified material reference 896/827 MPa; CP Grade 2 yield reference 275 MPa. No aerospace or medical qualification is claimed.

Relevant primary sources are linked directly on each material page. Product, industry, process, material and article links follow the application rather than using an unrelated link list. The existing site audience and inquiry infrastructure are unchanged.
