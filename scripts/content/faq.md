# Custom fastener FAQ

Answers to common purchasing and engineering questions before requesting drawing-defined metal screws. Product-specific decisions still follow the approved drawing, quantity and agreed acceptance requirements.

## Drawings and quotations

### What information is needed for a useful custom screw quotation?

Send the current drawing revision, quantity and expected repeat demand, application, required material and surface condition, critical dimensions and inspection or document requirements. Include the mating-part relationship when the screw locates, pivots, retains or seals with another component. If information is not yet fixed, identify the open questions rather than let each supplier make a different assumption.

### Can you work from a sample or a photograph?

A sample or clear photograph can help start a discussion, but it does not establish hidden geometry, material condition, tolerances or performance requirements. The team must clarify those details and confirm a controlled drawing or specification before production. A similar external appearance is not evidence that two parts will be interchangeable in the intended assembly.

### Is there one minimum order quantity for every custom part?

No single quantity is promised for all custom screws. Feasibility and the practical order quantity depend on geometry, material, tooling, route, treatment and the requested evidence. State the prototype quantity and expected production demand so that the team can review a suitable proposal. A minimum or economic quantity quoted for one design should not be applied to another without review.

## Samples and manufacturing

### How long do tooling and samples normally take?

Tooling and sampling are typically discussed at approximately 10–15 days, subject to drawing review and confirmation of the sample specification. Material availability, special treatment and required verification can affect the agreed schedule. Confirm the start milestone in the quotation. Repeat-production delivery is quoted separately and is not automatically the same as the sampling period.

### Should a custom screw be cold headed or CNC turned?

The route depends on the drawing, material, quantity and final acceptance requirements. Turning can be reviewed for complex geometry and flexible development quantities; forming or a hybrid route can be evaluated for suitable repeat-production programmes. Ask for route assumptions and a complete comparison including tooling, secondary operations, treatment and inspection. Neither process name alone proves a finished part will meet its requirements.

### Can the geometry change after sample approval?

Changes should follow a controlled review. Identify the affected drawing revision, functional interfaces, tooling, route and evidence. The customer’s responsible approver must accept a proposed change before it is treated as the production definition. A previous sample approval should not be interpreted as permission for an unreviewed substitution in material, treatment or geometry.

## Materials and surface treatment

### Does a material grade establish the finished screw strength?

A raw material designation is not, by itself, the complete finished-property requirement. Grade, material condition, manufacturing route and relevant treatment must be considered with the actual specification. State the required finished properties and evidence separately from the raw grade. Do not assume that every screw described with the same material family has the same delivered strength or service suitability.

### Can I request a different material if availability changes?

An alternative can be reviewed, but it must be approved against the actual interface and requirements. Compare the material condition, finished properties, treatment, dimensions, manufacturing route and documentation. A familiar cross-reference or a higher nominal strength does not automatically establish equivalence. Keep approved alternatives and any limitations identifiable on the drawing, order or deviation record.

### Which coating should I choose for outdoor equipment?

The correct review needs the actual exposure, mating materials, required protection, assembly behaviour and acceptance evidence. Colour alone is not a specification. Define treatment scope and final dimensions, especially for threads and close-fitting shoulders. A corrosion-test result on a loose fastener is not a universal prediction of field life for the installed joint.

## Inspection and acceptance

### Does CCD inspection check every property of a screw?

No. A suitable vision setup can check accessible dimensions and visible profiles within its agreed scope. It does not independently establish material strength, coating chemistry, hidden geometry or every three-dimensional relationship. Thread gauges, dimensional measurements, material or treatment records and any required assembly checks should be selected for the actual drawing and failure risks.

### Are all custom screws inspected on every characteristic on every piece?

Inspection scope and frequency must be agreed. First-article checks, lot sampling and every-piece sorting answer different needs. If every-piece checking is required, name the characteristic, method and criteria. Do not interpret an optical sort of selected external features as proof that every property of every part has been measured.

### Can you provide material and inspection documents?

Specify the exact documents and tests needed during quotation. Availability, traceability, testing arrangements and cost or schedule implications must be confirmed for the part. A generic data sheet is different from a record tied to a delivered lot. The website does not claim a certification, test result or document package that has not been confirmed for the actual order.

## Ordering, delivery and information

### How can repeat orders use the same approved specification?

Order against the controlled part number and drawing revision, retain the approved sample and acceptance record, and agree notification requirements for relevant changes. Identify material, finish, inspection, packaging and lot-label expectations. A repeat order should preserve the approved interface rather than rely only on a familiar part description or an old photograph.

### Does an application study on this website describe a delivered customer project?

Application studies are illustrative design reviews unless explicitly supported and identified otherwise. They explain how a team might connect an assembly need with geometry, materials, process and validation. They do not claim measured customer results, OEM approval or a specific delivered programme. ZUFEK’s automotive background refers to component supply-chain projects, not an unsupported direct OEM endorsement.
