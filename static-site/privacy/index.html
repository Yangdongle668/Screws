Privacy notice

How ZUFEK handles business inquiries and personal information.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who handles your information?

DONGGUAN ZUFEK TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD., 1st Floor, No. 353, Fuyuan District, Songmushan, Dalang Town, Dongguan City, Guangdong, China, is responsible for the inquiry information it receives. Contact engineer@zufek.com for privacy questions.

What we collect and why

When you submit an inquiry, we receive your name, business email, company, selected product, quantities, project requirements and optional drawing. We use these to assess feasibility, prepare a quotation and follow up. Please omit unrelated personal or sensitive information from drawings.

For EU/EEA contacts where GDPR applies, our usual basis is our legitimate interest in responding to relevant business requests and maintaining business records. Pre-contractual steps may apply when you personally are the prospective contracting party; legal obligations apply where required. We do not use an inquiry as consent to unrelated marketing. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Security and technical information

For abuse prevention, the service derives an hourly hash from the requesting IP address. Rate-limit records expire after two hours and are cleaned up on later submissions. The platform may also process IP addresses and technical logs to host and secure the service. We restrict access to inquiry records and drawings to authorized staff. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Recipients and international access

Website hosting, database and drawing storage use the Sites hosting infrastructure and Cloudflare services. Inquiry information is accessible to ZUFEK staff in China. If email notifications are enabled, the configured mail provider also processes delivery information and inquiry content. We do not sell inquiry information.

EU/EEA transfers and processor terms must be assessed against the actual hosting and mail arrangements. Do not assume that information stays in the EU or that a particular transfer safeguard has already been established. Contact us for the safeguards applicable to your request.

Retention

We review inactive, unconverted inquiries after 12 months and remove or anonymize information no longer needed. Records linked to orders, disputes or legal obligations may be retained for the applicable requirement. Drawing files follow the related inquiry's retention decision. This is a review policy, not an automatic deletion guarantee. Hosting backups and technical logs follow their applicable service retention arrangements.

Your choices and rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights have legal exceptions, and portability applies only in qualifying circumstances. You can complain to the relevant supervisory authority. We normally respond within one month where GDPR applies; permitted extensions will be explained.

How to make a data request. We may request proportionate verification before releasing or changing information. We do not make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects from inquiry submissions.

Optional analytics

With your consent, we count page views and inquiry events by date, page, referral source and approximate country. We do not retain complete IP addresses or create persistent visitor IDs in these statistics. Aggregate rows are retained for up to 90 days and cleaned on subsequent analytics events. Consent-based source details may be attached to a submitted inquiry and follow its retention policy. Security abuse limiting uses a separate two-hour hashed key. Counts cover consenting visitors only, may include missed or repeated requests, and are not a count of unique people.

Cookies and changes

Optional first-party analytics runs only after consent. Advertising tracking is not used. See our Cookie policy. Material changes will be reflected in this notice and its update date.
