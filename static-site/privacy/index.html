Inquiry privacy notice

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Information you submit

The quotation form collects your name, business email, company, product interest, quantity, project description and optional drawing. The service also processes a short-lived network identifier for submission rate limiting.

How it is used

Information is used to review your request, communicate with you, prepare a quotation and manage follow-up. Inquiry records are stored separately from email notifications. Drawings are accessible through an authorized management interface.

Service providers

The website uses Sites hosting with Cloudflare-based database and file storage. Email notifications may be processed by an email delivery provider when configured. Please avoid sending information that is unnecessary for the initial review.

Retention and requests

Contact engineer@zufek.com to request access, correction or deletion of your inquiry. Retention needs are reviewed according to the inquiry and any resulting business relationship. Do not submit confidential drawings until any necessary confidentiality arrangements have been agreed.
