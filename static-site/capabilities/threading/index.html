Thread Rolling, Cutting & Tapping

The thread profile, material and batch conditions determine whether rolling, cutting, grinding or an internal tapping process is appropriate.

Flat-die and cylindrical-die rolling

External threads are formed by displacement. Suitable material condition, blank diameter and tool geometry are essential. Machine capability sets the actual size range; no universal M0.8–M12 limit applies.

Single-point cutting and special profiles

Turning can address specified pitches, tapers and special profiles where tooling and access permit. NPT, MJ and other named threads require the applicable complete specification, not an approximate profile.

Internal cut and form tapping

Cut tapping removes material; form tapping displaces a sufficiently ductile material and needs the correct pre-hole. Neither method is universally stronger in every grade and geometry.

Acceptance and sequence

Define designation, class, usable length, runout and gauging. Rolling may improve fatigue behavior in suitable conditions, but is not a blanket strength guarantee. Review whether threading occurs before or after heat treatment.
