Where it fits

Prototypes, varied geometries, small batches and parts with several machined features. Actual batch economics depend on cycle time, stock and setup.

Swiss-type and fixed-head choices

Part length-to-diameter ratio, support and the required operations guide the choice. Turn-mill capability can combine turning, flats, holes and tapped features where the machine setup permits.

Design inputs

Provide bore depth, wall thickness, datum relationships, burr limits and surface finish. Access, tool reach and workholding remain feasibility constraints.

Cost and properties

Material removal increases chip generation and may increase unit cost. A machined part is not universally weaker than a formed part: grade, geometry, heat treatment and loading determine performance.
