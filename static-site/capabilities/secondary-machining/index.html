Secondary & Precision Operations

Local holes, flats, grooves and finished diameters often create the functional difference in a non-standard fastener.

Drilling, reaming and milling

Axial bores, radial holes, pin holes, flats, squares, drive recesses and local slots. Define position, edge breaks, hole exits and access.

Knurling, grooves and tip work

Straight or diamond knurls, circlip grooves, thread reliefs, oil grooves, tip cuts and controlled end shapes. Some features can be formed; others are machined.

Eccentric, angled and press operations

Offset turning, angled faces, undercuts, clinching and controlled crimping require an explicit setup and inspection plan.

Grinding and specialist routes

External, centerless or thread grinding may suit specific size, surface and form requirements. A g6 or h7 shaft tolerance does not mandate centerless grinding in every case. Wire EDM and laser operations are reviewed only where geometry and economics justify them.

Prepare a complete specification

Use the RFQ checklist, review materials and include the required inspection and documentation.
