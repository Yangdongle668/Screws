ILLUSTRATIVE PROCESS PLANNING

Three complete routes.
Defined by the drawing.

Use these examples to discuss the full chain. They are not fixed instructions: thread timing, heat treatment, grinding and coatings require material- and application-specific approval.

PROCESS ROUTE

A / Production shoulder screw

A formed blank with selected finishing operations.

  1. Wire / grade confirmation
  2. Cold heading of head and rough shoulder
  3. Secondary turning / reliefs
  4. Thread generation at the approved stage
  5. Specified heat treatment, if required
  6. Precision finishing of functional surfaces, if needed
  7. Cleaning and specified coating system
  8. Hydrogen-relief operation where required by the process
  9. Final dimensions, thread and agreed acceptance checks

PROCESS ROUTE

B / Small-batch eccentric or hollow part

Bar machining with local features and material-specific finishing.

  1. Bar stock / grade and condition
  2. CNC or Swiss-type turning
  3. Axial bore / cross holes / milled features
  4. Thread generation
  5. Material-specific stress relief or other treatment only if needed
  6. Deburring and controlled cleaning
  7. Specified passivation or coating, if applicable
  8. Agreed dimensional, functional and cleanliness checks

PROCESS ROUTE

C / Large forged eye component

A forged form followed by machining and verified mechanical condition.

  1. Approved load case and material specification
  2. Stock cutting and hot forging
  3. Trimming, scale removal and machining
  4. Specified heat treatment
  5. Thread generation in the approved sequence
  6. Specified finish and hydrogen-risk controls
  7. Agreed dimensional and mechanical verification
  8. Traceable documents and approval before intended lifting use

Decide the sequence before quoting.

Some threads are formed before heat treatment; other applications call for later thread generation. Final grinding and coating allowance must protect the specified fit. Hydrogen-relief baking belongs only where required and cannot be assumed for every zinc-flake or passivation route.
