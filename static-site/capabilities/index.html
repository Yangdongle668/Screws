Four ways to start.

ROUTE 01 / FORMING

Multi-Station Cold Heading ↗

Wire is cut and formed through a sequence of dies. Head, flange and some shoulder features can be integrated into a repeatable production route.

ROUTE 02 / BAR MACHINING

Swiss-Type & CNC Turning ↗

Bar-fed turning and turn-mill operations create drawing-specific steps, bores and other details with flexible programming.

ROUTE 03 / HEATED FORMING

Hot Heading & Forging ↗

Heated stock can be formed into larger or heavier geometries before finishing operations define the final interfaces.

ROUTE 04 / HYBRID

Cold Heading + Secondary Machining ↗

A formed blank provides the basic shape; selected machined features establish critical steps, faces or holes.

02 / COMPLETE THE PART

The operations beyond the blank.

PROCESS / THREAD GENERATION

Thread Rolling, Cutting & Tapping ↗

The thread profile, material and batch conditions determine whether rolling, cutting, grinding or an internal tapping process is appropriate.

PROCESS / FUNCTIONAL FEATURES

Secondary & Precision Operations ↗

Local holes, flats, grooves and finished diameters often create the functional difference in a non-standard fastener.

PROCESS / MATERIAL CONDITION

Material-Specific Heat Treatment ↗

Heat-treatment selection follows the alloy, required condition and applicable acceptance specification. One sequence cannot be applied to every screw.

PROCESS / SURFACE SYSTEM

Coatings & Surface Treatments ↗

Specify a complete surface system: substrate condition, coating, thickness, functional surfaces and acceptance criteria.

PROCESS / FINAL CONDITION

Cleaning, Locking & Assembly ↗

Cleanliness, burr control and pre-applied features are part of the finished-part specification, not an afterthought.

PROCESS / ACCEPTANCE

Inspection & Verification Planning ↗

Select checks by the drawing, failure risk and agreed acceptance requirements. Non-standard does not automatically mean every characteristic is inspected on every piece.
