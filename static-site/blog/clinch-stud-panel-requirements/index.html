Custom clinch studs: specify the panel as well as the fastener

A press-in connection depends on the panel, hole and installation process. A custom stud cannot be evaluated as an isolated threaded shape.

By Theo Wan • ZUFEK Technical Editorial • Published October 29, 2026

RELATED PRODUCTCustom Clinch Screws & Studs ↗

Provide the panel condition

Specify panel material, thickness, hardness where required and any finish present during installation. Include local geometry and access for the press tooling. These conditions influence how the sheet engages the retention features and how the finished connection can be verified.

Define the hole and retention geometry

Show hole dimensions, preparation and permitted edge condition. Define the stud undercut, serrations or other press-in features on the approved drawing. A resemblance to a standard proprietary part does not establish that the custom version has the same installation requirements or ratings.

Working on this type of part? Explore our custom clinch screws & studs and include the functional requirements in your RFQ.

Review tooling support and installation

Explain how the panel and stud will be supported during pressing. Avoid applying an installation condition copied from a different component without review. Agree sample assembly requirements so the proposed fastener and the actual panel can be evaluated together.

Specify acceptance beyond appearance

Where relevant, define push-out, torque-out or other functional checks with methods and criteria. A flush-looking head does not by itself prove retention. The quotation should distinguish dimensional inspection of the stud from qualification of the pressed panel assembly.

Specification example

For a custom stud in a coated panel, state whether coating occurs before or after installation. That sequence can change the fit and the inspection conditions and should be resolved before production.

RFQ checklist

  • Panel material and thickness
  • Hole preparation and dimensions
  • Stud retention features
  • Press support and sample assembly
  • Functional retention tests
Ready to discuss your part?

Use the RFQ preparation checklist, then submit your drawing for review. For acceptance requirements, see quality planning.

General guidance only. Material properties, tolerances, standards and manufacturing feasibility must be verified for the actual specification. This article does not establish a product performance guarantee.
