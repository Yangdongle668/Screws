Hollow screw bores: the details an RFQ should include

An axial hole changes both manufacturing and inspection. A useful hollow-screw specification explains the passage, the surrounding wall and the conditions inside the bore.

By Theo Wan • ZUFEK Technical Editorial • Published October 29, 2026

Describe the entire passage

Identify whether the hole is through, blind, stepped or connected to a side passage. Dimension each section and its axial position. A single opening diameter does not define an internal path, and a photograph cannot establish hidden counterbores or restrictions.

Review wall thickness with the thread

The bore and external thread share the same material section. Show their relationship through the thinnest region and identify any concentricity or position requirement. Manufacturing review needs the actual geometry; an outside diameter alone is not sufficient to evaluate a hollow threaded feature.

Working on this type of part? Explore our hollow & through-hole screws and include the functional requirements in your RFQ.

State edge and cleanliness requirements

Drilling can leave burrs at hole exits and intersections. Specify which edges must be broken and how internal burrs will be accepted. If the part carries fluid, adhesive or a wire, explain the relevant contamination, surface-damage or blockage concern rather than relying on a generic clean note.

Define functional tests separately

A continuous bore does not itself prove pressure capability, leak tightness or unrestricted flow in an assembly. State any test medium, fixture arrangement and acceptance method that the application requires. These conditions should be agreed before sampling so manufacturing and inspection can be quoted together.

Specification example

For a screw used as a cable passage, show the cable envelope and the edges it contacts. For a fluid passage, describe the intended sealing interface separately from the through hole.

RFQ checklist

  • Complete bore section
  • Minimum wall and feature relationship
  • Hole exit and intersection burr limits
  • Internal cleanliness requirements
  • Required functional test method
