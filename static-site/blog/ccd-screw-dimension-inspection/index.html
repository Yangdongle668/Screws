What CCD vision inspection can check on a custom screw

Camera-based dimensional inspection can evaluate visible features of a screw, but the image, setup and inspection program must match the requirement. It is one part of a complete acceptance plan.

By Theo Wan • ZUFEK Technical Editorial • Published October 29, 2026

Choose the visible features to measure

Overall length, head diameter and stepped external profiles can be useful targets for optical checks when they are presented clearly. Identify the actual drawing characteristics rather than requesting a generic camera inspection with no defined measurements or acceptance limits.

Review presentation and lighting

Stable support, suitable optics and controlled lighting help produce consistent edges. Backlighting is useful for silhouette-based checks, while other surface features may require a different lighting arrangement. Establish the inspection view for the part rather than assuming one image reveals every characteristic.

Working on this type of part? Explore our shoulder & step screws and include the functional requirements in your RFQ.

Recognize what the image does not establish

A visible outline does not prove material grade, hardness or internal features hidden from the camera. Thread functional acceptance may require the specified gauges or other methods. The inspection plan should identify complementary checks instead of presenting one camera station as complete evidence of every requirement.

Validate and maintain the inspection program

Agree the measurement setup, acceptance limits, reference parts and any checks used to confirm consistent operation. Document changes to part revision or finish that may affect the image. Inspection coverage and handling of rejected parts should be defined for the actual order.

Apply the guidance to the assembly

Select the route and verify the result. CNC turning is a route to review for sample quantities and complex steps. Cold heading followed by local machining can be evaluated for repeat production. Grinding is selected where size, form or surface requirements justify it, rather than assumed from the product name. CCD inspection can screen accessible profiles; datum relationships, surface texture and thread acceptance need their own suitable methods.

Validate what the camera can actually see

Select representative parts and review their presentation, contrast, orientation and edge visibility in the intended inspection setup. Compare the measurement with an appropriate reference method for the specified characteristic. Establish the program revision and handling of borderline or rejected parts. A screen showing pass does not demonstrate that an obscured surface, internal bore or material property has been inspected.

Specification example

For a shoulder screw, define head diameter, shoulder length and overall length as separate checks. Then specify how the thread and any internal drive requirements are verified outside that silhouette measurement.

RFQ checklist

  • Drawing characteristics and limits
  • Required views and presentation
  • Calibration and reference checks
  • Complementary gauges or tests
  • Coverage and reject-handling plan
