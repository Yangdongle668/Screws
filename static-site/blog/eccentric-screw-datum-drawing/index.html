Eccentric screw drawings: making the offset unambiguous

An eccentric feature needs a clear reference axis. Without a datum scheme, two manufacturers can interpret an apparently simple offset drawing differently.

By Theo Wan • ZUFEK Technical Editorial • Published October 29, 2026

Identify the functional axis

Show which shank, shoulder or thread establishes the assembly reference. Dimension the eccentric feature relative to that reference rather than an arbitrary drawing edge. If the thread does not locate the part in service, explain which smooth feature actually does.

Define offset and angular orientation

Specify the amount of eccentricity and any required orientation to a slot, flat or drive. An offset without angular control may be acceptable for one adjuster and unsuitable for another. Include the intended adjustment positions and how they will be identified during assembly.

Working on this type of part? Explore our eccentric & angled head screws and include the functional requirements in your RFQ.

Describe the mating contact

Provide the follower, bore or surface moved by the eccentric feature. Show clearances and travel limits. The assembly determines whether a geometric change produces the desired adjustment; the screw drawing alone cannot establish the motion of every connected component.

Choose an inspection setup that follows function

The inspection plan should establish the same reference features used in the design. Discuss the practical measurement of offset, form and orientation before production. A photograph can illustrate the concept but is not an adequate substitute for controlled datum and dimensional requirements.

Specification example

For a camera-module adjuster, show the feature that contacts the module and the surface that locates the screw. This helps distinguish the adjustment geometry from incidental machining surfaces.

RFQ checklist

  • Functional locating axis
  • Offset and orientation
  • Mating contact and travel
  • Critical datum relationships
  • Agreed measurement setup
