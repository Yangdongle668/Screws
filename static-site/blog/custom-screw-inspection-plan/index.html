How to prepare an inspection plan for custom screws

An inspection plan translates a drawing into agreed checks and records. Define it during the quotation stage so acceptance is understood before production.

ZUFEK Technical Resources • Published October 29, 2026
IN THIS GUIDEUse the current drawingIdentify critical characteristicsAgree on methods and recordsPlan sample approval
RELATED PRODUCTShoulder Screws ↗

Use the current drawing

Link the inspection plan to the drawing number and revision. Resolve unclear notes and conflicting requirements before the part is released.

Identify critical characteristics

List the dimensions, thread requirements, material and finish characteristics to be verified. Distinguish dimensional checks from functional assembly checks.

Working on this type of part? Explore our shoulder screws and include the functional requirements in your RFQ.

Agree on methods and records

Discuss measurement methods, sampling requirements and required reporting. Equipment suitability and acceptance criteria should be agreed for the actual features involved.

Plan sample approval

Where required, confirm how samples will be identified, reviewed and approved. Record approved changes so repeat orders refer to the same specification.

Ready to discuss your part?

Use the RFQ preparation checklist, then submit your drawing for review. For acceptance requirements, see quality planning.

General guidance only. Material properties, tolerances, standards and manufacturing feasibility must be verified for the actual specification. This article does not establish a product performance guarantee.
