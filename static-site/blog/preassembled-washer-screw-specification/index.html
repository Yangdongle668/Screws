Preassembled washer screws: specify the complete set

A preassembled screw is supplied as a small assembly. The washer, retention method and component sequence need the same control as the screw itself.

By Theo Wan • ZUFEK Technical Editorial • Published October 29, 2026

Define every supplied component

Identify the screw, plain or spring washer, seal or other retained element by drawing or controlled specification. Include the material and finish of each component. A general note to add a washer can leave dimensions and properties unresolved.

Show the stack order and retention

Provide a section or assembly view showing the sequence and which elements are retained before installation. Explain the required freedom of movement. The manufacturing route should establish how components are assembled and held without damaging threads or functional surfaces.

Working on this type of part? Explore our custom flange screws and include the functional requirements in your RFQ.

Review treatment and material compatibility

Heat treatment, coating and cleaning may need to occur before or after assembly depending on the components. Seals and coated washers can introduce restrictions. Confirm the sequence for the approved set rather than assuming a process suitable for the bare screw is suitable for every assembled element.

Inspect the delivered assembly

Acceptance can include component presence, correct order, retention and the specified dimensional or functional checks. Keep these separate from the screw thread inspection. Packaging should protect the assembly condition and make it practical to confirm that the complete set was supplied.

Specification example

For a screw carrying an O-ring and washer, provide the assembled section and the permitted retention condition. This allows the quotation to account for both the metal part and the handling of the sealing element.

RFQ checklist

  • Component list and specifications
  • Stack order and movement
  • Retention method
  • Treatment and cleaning sequence
  • Delivered-assembly acceptance
