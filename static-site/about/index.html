OUR BACKGROUND

Built around the details of your part.

Since 2005, ZUFEK has focused on custom metal fasteners where a standard catalog part does not fully answer the drawing. Our work centers on the details that affect assembly: a locating shoulder, a special drive, a through bore, an unusual thread or several functional features combined in one component.

Our Dalang location places the business within Dongguan’s manufacturing community. We work with customers on drawing clarification, sample requirements and repeat production, keeping material, geometry, finish and acceptance requirements connected throughout the quotation process.

Our production capacity is on the order of several million parts per month. Actual output, order allocation and delivery timing depend on part complexity, material, tooling, secondary operations and the agreed inspection scope.
