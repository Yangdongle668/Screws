SUS304 & SUS316 Stainless Steel

Austenitic stainless choices for parts where corrosion exposure, surface condition and the specified fastener strength class need to be considered together.

Strength reference

A2/A4 groups and classes such as 70 or 80 describe finished stainless fasteners under the applicable specification. SUS304 or SUS316 alone does not establish A2-70 or A4-80 performance.

Values refer to the stated material or fastener-class basis. They are not a certified property or load rating for every ZUFEK custom geometry. Confirm the applicable specification and test condition for the ordered part.

Why consider this family?

  • Useful for corrosion-exposed equipment and accessible exterior hardware
  • Grade and finish can be chosen for the actual medium
  • Commonly discussed for machined and custom-formed screw geometries

Application range

Equipment covers, electronic and optical housings, outdoor security hardware and selected fluid-handling connections.

Selection and process considerations

304 and 316 are not precipitation-hardening grades. Review the actual environment rather than assuming any stainless part is corrosion-proof. Consider thread galling, mating metals, cleaning and passivation. Form and final condition affect the mechanical requirements.

Include in the RFQ

  • 304 or 316 grade requirement
  • Finished fastener class where applicable
  • Exposure medium and mating metals
  • Surface condition, passivation and thread acceptance
