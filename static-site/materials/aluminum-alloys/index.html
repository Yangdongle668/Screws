Aluminum Alloys

Lightweight metal options for custom threaded parts where the alloy, temper, engagement and surface requirements can be engineered for the application.

Why consider this family?

  • Lower component mass than an equivalent-volume steel part
  • Machined geometry can combine steps, bores and adjustment features
  • Surface finish and alloy temper can be reviewed together

Application range

Lightweight electronic assemblies, optical adjustment hardware, equipment covers and custom low-mass fixture components.

Selection and process considerations

Specify alloy and temper. Review thread stripping and engagement in the real joint, contact with dissimilar metals, wear, finishing allowance and assembly torque. Stock tensile properties do not by themselves establish the load capacity of a threaded component.

Include in the RFQ

  • Alloy, temper and stock form
  • Mating thread and engagement
  • Surface finish or anodizing requirement
  • Joint load and assembly conditions

Read the strength data in the right condition

A material stock value is not automatically the load rating of a screw or assembled joint. Keep the grade, product form, condition and applicable specification attached to the value when comparing alternatives. Review the thread engagement and mating material independently of the stock tensile figure.

Specify alloy and temper. Review thread stripping and engagement in the real joint, contact with dissimilar metals, wear, finishing allowance and assembly torque. Stock tensile properties do not by themselves establish the load capacity of a threaded component.

A practical selection and approval sequence

For Aluminum Alloys, first define alloy, temper and stock form; mating thread and engagement; surface finish or anodizing requirement; joint load and assembly conditions. Compare those requirements with the service environment and the functional geometry. If the starting requirement is only a material family, resolve the grade and condition before comparing final quotations.

Review the chosen material with Swiss-Type & CNC Turning, Thread Rolling, Cutting & Tapping, Coatings & Surface Treatments. Agree what evidence is needed at sample approval and what must accompany repeat batches. A substitute grade or condition requires approval; a similar appearance or nominal strength is not sufficient evidence of interchangeability.
