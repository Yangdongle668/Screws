Information that changes the choice

  • Environment, contact metals and operating temperature
  • Required strength, hardness and mechanical acceptance
  • Wear, friction, electrical or appearance requirements
  • Material traceability and required documents

Keep the grade and geometry together.

Availability, machinability, forming behavior and finish compatibility are checked for the proposed drawing. No blanket claim is made that every feature is achievable in every material.
