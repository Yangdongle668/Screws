Select around the application.

  • Required mechanical properties and joint function
  • Corrosion exposure, temperature and mating metals
  • Mass, conductivity, wear and appearance priorities
  • Manufacturing route, coating and thread engagement
  • Material evidence and final-part verification

Material is one part of the route.

A stronger stock material does not automatically produce a stronger connection. Head geometry, thread engagement, section changes, surface condition and the mating component still need review.
