Carbon & Alloy Steel

A practical choice for drawing-defined fasteners where the required mechanical class, wear behavior and surface protection can be specified together.

Strength reference

Specify the finished fastener property class, not a strength inferred from the raw grade. Common nominal class references include 8.8, 10.9 and 12.9.

Grade / condition / basisTensile strength Rm (MPa)Yield / 0.2% stress (MPa)Scope of the reference
8.8 nominal class reference800640Nominal designation values; actual standard minimums depend on applicability and size.
10.9 nominal class reference1,000900Nominal designation values, not a substitute for the acceptance table.
12.9 nominal class reference1,2001,080Nominal designation values; review geometry, material and final condition.

Values refer to the stated material or fastener-class basis. They are not a certified property or load rating for every ZUFEK custom geometry. Confirm the applicable specification and test condition for the ordered part.

Why consider this family?

  • Broad choice of grades and final mechanical conditions
  • Suitable route choices include forming and machining, subject to grade
  • Surface protection can be matched to the environment

Application range

Automotive component brackets, machinery connections, fixture shoulder screws and application-specific clamping.

Selection and process considerations

Steel grade, heat treatment and property class are different specifications. Review corrosion exposure, hydrogen-related process risks where applicable, coating friction and dimensional allowances. Reduced-load geometries need their own assessment.

Include in the RFQ

  • Grade and final property requirement
  • Applicable fastener specification and size
  • Heat-treatment and coating system
  • Functional load case and acceptance tests
