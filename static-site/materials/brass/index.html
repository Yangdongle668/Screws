Brass

Copper-zinc alloys can be useful for drawing-specific machined threaded components where machining behavior, contact conditions and the selected alloy composition fit the application.

Strength reference

Strength changes with composition and temper. The example below is published typical H02 rod data and must not be applied to all brass fasteners.

Grade / condition / basisTensile strength Rm (MPa)Yield / 0.2% stress (MPa)Scope of the reference
C36000 rod, H02 reference386 typical310 typicalCDA typical rod values; use the ordered alloy/temper specification for acceptance.

Values refer to the stated material or fastener-class basis. They are not a certified property or load rating for every ZUFEK custom geometry. Confirm the applicable specification and test condition for the ordered part.

Why consider this family?

  • Useful for custom turned profiles and threaded details
  • Alloy selection can support different appearance and contact requirements
  • Material composition can be matched to the customer specification

Application range

Instrument hardware, adjustment components, connection parts and selected custom fittings.

Selection and process considerations

Specify the actual alloy rather than a brass color. C36000 is a leaded free-cutting brass; composition or regulatory restrictions may require a different alloy. Mechanical behavior, media compatibility and any required leak test remain application-specific.

Include in the RFQ

  • Exact alloy and composition restrictions
  • Temper and material documentation
  • Threaded and contact geometry
  • Finish and functional acceptance
