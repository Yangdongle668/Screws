Why consider this family?

  • Useful for custom turned profiles and threaded details

Application range

Instrument hardware, adjustment components, connection parts and selected custom fittings.

Selection and process considerations

Specify the actual alloy rather than a brass color. C36000 is a leaded free-cutting brass; composition or regulatory restrictions may require a different alloy. Mechanical behavior, media compatibility and any required leak test remain application-specific.

Include in the RFQ

  • Exact alloy and composition restrictions
  • Temper and material documentation
  • Threaded and contact geometry
  • Finish and functional acceptance

A material stock value is not automatically the load rating of a screw or assembled joint. Keep the grade, product form, condition and applicable specification attached to the value when comparing alternatives. Review the thread engagement and mating material independently of the stock tensile figure.

A practical selection and approval sequence

For Brass, first define exact alloy and composition restrictions; temper and material documentation; threaded and contact geometry; finish and functional acceptance. Compare those requirements with the service environment and the functional geometry. If the starting requirement is only a material family, resolve the grade and condition before comparing final quotations.

Review the chosen material with Swiss-Type & CNC Turning, Thread Rolling, Cutting & Tapping, CCD Vision & Dimensional Inspection. Agree what evidence is needed at sample approval and what must accompany repeat batches. A substitute grade or condition requires approval; a similar appearance or nominal strength is not sufficient evidence of interchangeability.
