10B21 Boron Steel

A boron-alloyed steel grade considered for formed fastener routes when the drawing, final material condition and required properties are defined.

Strength reference

10B21 is a grade designation, not a finished strength class. There is no single tensile strength that applies to every 10B21 screw.

Read the strength data in the right condition

10B21 is a grade designation, not a finished strength class. There is no single tensile strength that applies to every 10B21 screw. A material stock value is not automatically the load rating of a screw or assembled joint. Keep the grade, product form, condition and applicable specification attached to the value when comparing alternatives. Review the thread engagement and mating material independently of the stock tensile figure.

Do not prescribe carburizing solely because the drawing says 10B21. State the required final mechanical properties and any case-depth or hardness requirements where relevant. Confirm the actual grade, processing route and material documentation during quotation.

A practical selection and approval sequence

For 10B21 Boron Steel, first define exact grade and material condition; required finished properties; core or case requirements where applicable; treatment records and mechanical acceptance. Compare those requirements with the service environment and the functional geometry. If the starting requirement is only a material family, resolve the grade and condition before comparing final quotations.

Review the chosen material with Multi-Station Cold Heading, Material-Specific Heat Treatment, CCD Vision & Dimensional Inspection. Agree what evidence is needed at sample approval and what must accompany repeat batches. A substitute grade or condition requires approval; a similar appearance or nominal strength is not sufficient evidence of interchangeability.
