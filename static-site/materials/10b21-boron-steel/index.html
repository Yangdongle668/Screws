10B21 Boron Steel

A boron-alloyed steel grade considered for formed fastener routes when the drawing, final material condition and required properties are defined.

Strength reference

10B21 is a grade designation, not a finished strength class. There is no single tensile strength that applies to every 10B21 screw.

Values refer to the stated material or fastener-class basis. They are not a certified property or load rating for every ZUFEK custom geometry. Confirm the applicable specification and test condition for the ordered part.

Why consider this family?

  • Relevant to repeat fastener forming discussions
  • Can be reviewed with the complete heat-treatment route
  • Final requirements can distinguish core properties and surface condition

Application range

Drawing-defined production screws, flange parts and selected high-volume automotive or equipment fastener applications.

Selection and process considerations

Do not prescribe carburizing solely because the drawing says 10B21. State the required final mechanical properties and any case-depth or hardness requirements where relevant. Confirm the actual grade, processing route and material documentation during quotation.

Include in the RFQ

  • Exact grade and material condition
  • Required finished properties
  • Core or case requirements where applicable
  • Treatment records and mechanical acceptance
