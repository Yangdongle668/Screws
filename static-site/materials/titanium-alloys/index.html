Titanium & Titanium Alloys

Titanium grades are considered where mass, corrosion exposure and required mechanical properties justify a grade-specific manufacturing and verification plan.

Strength reference

Commercially pure Grade 2 and Ti-6Al-4V are different materials. Grade, product form and condition must accompany any strength value.

Values refer to the stated material or fastener-class basis. They are not a certified property or load rating for every ZUFEK custom geometry. Confirm the applicable specification and test condition for the ordered part.

Why consider this family?

  • Useful strength-to-mass considerations for suitable grades
  • Corrosion-driven material selection in appropriate environments
  • Custom turned features can be evaluated against the specific grade

Application range

Lightweight equipment, optical and precision assemblies, and selected corrosion-exposed custom connections.

Selection and process considerations

A titanium label does not imply an aerospace or medical qualification. Review grade, final condition, thread galling, surface treatment, mating metals and verification. Exact mechanical and traceability requirements need agreement before production.

Include in the RFQ

  • Exact titanium grade and condition
  • Required mechanical properties
  • Surface and assembly requirements
  • Traceability and verification scope
