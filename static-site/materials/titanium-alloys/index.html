How to narrow the choice

First decide whether commercially pure titanium or an alloy is needed. Then specify the exact grade, product standard, condition, surface requirements and verification. Review thread galling and mating surfaces during assembly planning.

Write an unambiguous material requirement

Include the designation standard, exact grade, product form, delivery condition, final mechanical requirements, surface treatment and acceptance evidence. Similar names across EN, ASTM, SAE, JIS or GB systems are comparison leads, not permission to substitute.

If the grade is not yet decided, send the load or locating function, environment, mating materials, quantity and drawing. Ask for a proposed grade and route with stated assumptions before approving the material.

The listed grades are comparisons for drawing review, not a declaration that every grade, size or treatment is held in stock. Availability, route and ordered evidence are confirmed in the quotation.

Selection, processing and acceptance

Choose the grade and condition together with the job the screw performs. The following checks connect the material shortlist to manufacturing and sample acceptance.

Function and grade choice
Choose commercially pure Grade 2 when its corrosion and mechanical balance suits the duty; compare Grade 5 when the design calls for an alloy-strength route. Grade 23 ELI has its own interstitial limits and specification. Selecting it does not by itself approve a medical or aerospace component.
Processing and final dimensions
Grade and condition affect cutting, tool wear and treatment planning. Review the final thread and bearing interfaces for galling, and specify compatible lubrication or surface treatment where needed. Do not import a steel tightening torque or heat-treatment instruction into a titanium drawing without an assembly-specific review.
What the sample must demonstrate
State product standard, grade, condition and required lot traceability. Keep stock property records separate from final dimensions and any ordered mechanical tests. If mass, fatigue, corrosion or repeated adjustment drives the choice, define how that function will be verified in the actual geometry and assembly.

Material selection questions

Can Grade 2 and Grade 5 use the same strength value?

No. Grade 2 is commercially pure titanium; Grade 5 is Ti-6Al-4V. Compare the specified product form and condition and use the appropriate property basis for each.

Does Grade 23 ELI mean the screw is medically approved?

No. ELI identifies a material variant. Application qualification, manufacturing controls, traceability and acceptance of the finished device remain separate requirements.

Why consider this family?

  • Useful strength-to-mass considerations for suitable grades
  • Corrosion-driven material selection in appropriate environments
  • Custom turned features can be evaluated against the specific grade

Application range

Lightweight equipment, optical and precision assemblies, and selected corrosion-exposed custom connections.

Selection and process considerations

A titanium label does not imply an aerospace or medical qualification. Review grade, final condition, thread galling, surface treatment, mating metals and verification. Exact mechanical and traceability requirements need agreement before production.

Include in the RFQ

  • Exact titanium grade and condition
  • Required mechanical properties
  • Surface and assembly requirements
  • Traceability and verification scope
