ZUFEK TECHNICAL EDITORIAL

Theo Wan 万天恩

Theo Wan is the editorial pen name used for ZUFEK’s practical guides on custom fastener specifications, manufacturing choices, inspection and procurement.

Focus of the articles

The guides help engineers and sourcing teams prepare clearer drawings and RFQs. Topics include shoulder and bore requirements, special drives, manufacturing routes, final material condition, surface treatment and acceptance planning.

Articles provide general technical and purchasing guidance. Application-specific design, safety requirements and manufacturing feasibility need review against the actual approved specification.

Editorial approach

Published under an editorial pen name. Each guide focuses on information engineers and buyers can use to prepare drawings, compare manufacturing options and agree acceptance requirements.

Sources are linked where useful. Specification examples illustrate a discussion point and are not presented as customer case histories or test results.
