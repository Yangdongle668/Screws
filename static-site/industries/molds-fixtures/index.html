Knurled thumb screws: specifying grip and adjustment ↗

A hand-adjusted screw must be accessible, comfortable to grip and compatible with the required adjustment. Head texture alone does not define how the mechanism works.

Multi-step screws: controlling the axial dimension chain ↗
