Translate the application into a fastener specification

For general machinery & equipment, the first task is to connect the fastener geometry with the equipment interface. Mating connection details: Provide both thread interfaces, mounting slots or linkage pins that determine the required geometry. Assembly and load requirements: Define installation sequence, material condition and required verification for the complete connection. Repeat-order consistency: Keep drawing revisions, sample decisions, inspection scope and shipment identification aligned. Explain the environment and service sequence alongside the drawing so the review addresses the installed component.

For a mixed-thread connection, identify end A and end B on the drawing and inspection plan. If the application involves lifting, provide the approved design and verification requirements separately.

Select a route around the functional features

The relevant product families include Custom Double-Ended Studs, Custom Square Head Bolts, Custom T-Slot Bolts, Custom Eye & Articulating Bolts. These are starting points for a drawing review, not interchangeable parts. Compare Carbon & Alloy Steel, 10B21 Boron Steel, SUS304 & SUS316 Stainless Steel against the actual mechanical and environmental requirements.

Discuss Hot Heading & Forging, Thread Rolling, Cutting & Tapping, CCD Vision & Dimensional Inspection as part of the complete route. Identify sample quantity, critical characteristics, finish and required records before approval. A sector name alone does not imply a project certification or a blanket test scope.

Plan sample approval and repeat orders

Typical sampling, including tooling, takes approximately 10–15 days, subject to technical review and confirmation. Confirm the schedule basis, sample acceptance and any mating-part trial in the quotation. Release repeat production against the approved revision and record changes to material, treatment or geometry rather than relying on a previous photograph.

ENGINEERING GUIDE

Eye bolts for linkages and lifting: different specification needs ↗

An eye-shaped end can belong to a linkage, a tensioning device or a lifting component. Similar appearance does not establish the same design requirements or approved use.

ENGINEERING GUIDE

T-slot bolt fit: what to measure before ordering ↗

A T-head must enter, turn or slide inside the intended slot while retaining the required contact. The slot cross-section is therefore an essential part of the fastener RFQ.
