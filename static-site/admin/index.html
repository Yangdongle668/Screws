    Full backups include inquiry records, follow-up notes, uploaded drawings, article drafts, publication history and aggregated analytics. Download while records are not being edited. CSV is a separate lightweight export. These are manual copies, not a scheduled independent backup. Backups contain confidential customer data: store them in restricted encrypted storage.
