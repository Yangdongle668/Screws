Shoulder & Step Screws

A head, a locating shoulder and a threaded section combine fastening with controlled spacing or movement.

Calculate the shoulder clearance from both tolerance limits ↓

Check the bore fit separately from the axial clearance

The shoulder-to-bore interface guides movement; the distance between the seating faces controls the axial stack. Define both in the finished condition rather than treating a thread fit as the locating fit.

  1. Shoulder and mating bore: specify the two limits, the reference axis and any functional surface requirement.
  2. Seating and transition: show the mating chamfer or counterbore so the incomplete thread or relief does not prevent seating.
  3. After finishing: identify the surfaces affected by treatment or coating and the stage at which the final fit is checked.

Read the clearance calculation below alongside these interfaces. A satisfactory axial gap does not by itself establish the bore fit or the required geometric relationship.

Calculate the shoulder clearance from both tolerance limits

For a moving plate, shoulder length must be compared with the complete stack between the seating faces. Include the washer, bushing and plate in that stack; overall screw length does not determine movement clearance.

Assume a shoulder length L of 8.20 ± 0.03 mm and a complete moving stack S of 8.00 ± 0.05 mm. The illustrative required axial clearance is 0.10–0.30 mm. Both dimensions refer to the final supplied condition.

Common specification questions

Can the shoulder and thread have different diameters?

Yes, the shoulder and thread are specified independently. Review the transition, available mating clearance and effective thread engagement as one geometry.

Is every shoulder screw ground?

No. The final size, form and surface requirements determine the finishing route. Review the need for grinding with treatment distortion and the final acceptance condition.

How is a shoulder screw different from a fully threaded bolt?

A shoulder screw has an unthreaded bearing or locating section between its head and thread. Select that shoulder for the required rotation, location or spacing function, and specify thread engagement separately. A fully threaded bolt does not provide the same smooth bearing surface.

How do I choose the shoulder length without clamping a moving part?

Review the complete axial stack, including washers and the mating seat. The shoulder must seat as intended while preserving the specified movement clearance. Show the assembly and tolerance stack on the drawing; nominal shoulder length alone cannot establish the final clearance.
