Custom Flange Screws

An integral flange provides a drawing-defined bearing footprint without a separately handled washer.

DRAWING-DEFINED FEATURES
01

Flange dimensions

Customize diameter, thickness, flatness and under-head transition.

02

Bearing face

Plain, textured or serrated faces require different mating-surface review.

03

Thin or enlarged flange

Balance footprint, stiffness and available assembly clearance.

Where this design fits

Equipment covers, brackets and assemblies requiring a controlled bearing area.

What to include in the RFQ

Include the mating material and bearing-face requirements. A larger flange does not automatically prevent surface damage.

Material and acceptance

Carbon steel, 10B21, SUS304/316, aluminum, copper/brass and titanium alloy requirements can be discussed. Suitability is specific to the part, grade and process.

Define material condition, coating, critical dimensions and required inspection records. A named product family does not imply a tested load, pressure or performance rating.
