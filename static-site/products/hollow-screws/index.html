Internal finish

Where this design fits

Cable passages, dispensing fixtures, pneumatic routing and custom fluid-handling assemblies.

What to include in the RFQ

State bore diameter and depth, permitted burrs, cleanliness and any agreed leak or pressure test. A through hole alone does not establish a pressure rating.
