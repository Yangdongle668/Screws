Common specification questions

Can CCD inspect the complete bore?

External silhouette inspection cannot establish the full internal passage. Bore geometry and internal edges need suitable complementary checks.

Are hollow screws pressure-rated?

A through hole does not create a pressure rating. Material, sealing design, service conditions and the required verification must be specified for the application.

Follow the smallest wall and the hidden bore edge

Use a section view to connect the passage to the outside features. The critical remaining wall may be beside a thread root or an intersecting hole rather than at the largest outside diameter.

  1. Thread root to bore: define the remaining section using the applicable thread geometry and the bore position.
  2. Hole intersection: identify the internal edge, burr requirement and a workable deburring and inspection route.
  3. Finished passage: agree bore clearance, entry/exit condition and cleaning before assembly makes the internal features inaccessible.

External CCD screening can support visible profile checks. Verify inaccessible bore features with a suitable separate method, and use the application matrix below to distinguish cable, flow and sealing requirements.

Specify the passage, not just the outside screw

A cable feed-through, an air passage and a pressure-retaining connection need different acceptance checks even when the outside screw looks identical. State what passes through the bore and which surfaces perform the sealing or locating function.

ApplicationWhat to specifyWhat to check

Review the smallest remaining wall at the thread root and at intersecting holes. Plan how drilling burrs can be removed and checked before a later operation hides them. For internal surfaces, agree a suitable access or inspection method instead of treating external CCD screening as complete bore inspection.

Connect bore finishing, cleaning and inspection

Tooling and sampling typically take approximately 10–15 days after technical review and confirmation. Confirm the starting milestone, sample acceptance and repeat-production schedule in the quotation.
