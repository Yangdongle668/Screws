Pitch, travel and locking

Relate pitch to the intended adjustment, while considering the mating thread fit and available engagement. Fine pitch does not by itself establish positioning accuracy or vibration resistance. State contact geometry, travel, locking and thread acceptance as separate requirements.

From drawing to approved samples

Provide the drawing, material, finish and intended assembly. Identify the drawing revision, prototype quantity and expected repeat demand. Where the first part is based on an existing sample, separately confirm material and hidden features rather than inferring them from appearance.

The typical sampling lead time, including tooling, is approximately 10–15 days. The schedule is confirmed after drawing review and agreement on the sample specification; material availability, special treatment and inspection requirements can affect the confirmed date. Confirm when the agreed schedule starts in the quotation. Sample approval and repeat-production delivery are separate milestones.
