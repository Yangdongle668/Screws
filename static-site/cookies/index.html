Cookie & storage policy

Choose essential-only browsing or allow first-party analytics.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What is active?

We offer optional first-party analytics, disabled until you consent. We use no advertising pixels or embedded third-party videos. Fonts use the browser's local system fonts, so loading a page does not request Google Fonts.

StoragePurpose and duration
zufek_privacy_v2
First-party localStorage		Remembers your essential and analytics choices. Contains the policy version and expiry, not your inquiry content. Expires in our application after 180 days; overwritten when you save again. You can remove it through your browser's site-data controls.
zufek_analytics
First-party cookie		Stores 1 or 0 so the service can enforce your analytics choice. Up to 180 days, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS. No visitor identifier.
zufek_attribution_v1
First-party sessionStorage		With analytics consent only: remembers landing page, referral domain and limited campaign parameters during this browser tab session. Cleared on withdrawal and when the tab session ends. Used to attribute a submitted inquiry.
Hosting sign-in sessionThe hosting platform may use necessary authentication cookies when sign-in is required, including restricted previews and administration. These are managed by the platform; actual names and durations depend on its authentication session.

The inquiry retry identifier exists in page memory, not a tracking cookie. Contact fields and drawings are not saved to localStorage. Optional attribution is stored separately only after consent. Server-side inquiry records are described in the Privacy notice.

Your manual language choice is stored as zufek_language in localStorage until you clear it. zufek_language_visit in sessionStorage prevents repeated automatic switching during the tab session. These preferences do not identify visitors or enable analytics.

Control your settings

Analytics is optional. Accept all enables the analytics currently offered; marketing is not in use. Reject optional disables analytics. Save preferences applies your selected choice. You can withdraw through the footer at any time. Browser settings can clear site storage; blocking all storage may affect necessary functionality.
