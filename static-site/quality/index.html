Full inspection where it is justified.

Agree which features need 100% screening and which use an approved sampling plan. Full dimensional screening does not replace material or destructive test requirements.

CCD optical dimensional inspection, thread gauges and mechanical testing address different characteristics. Non-contact silhouette measurement supports accessible external features; internal geometry and other requirements need appropriate complementary methods. Availability and any external laboratory arrangements are confirmed in the quotation.

Precision means a defined requirement.

Specify the fit and surface condition rather than assuming one machining method is mandatory. Distinguish shaft fits such as g6/h7 from thread classes such as 6g/6H.
