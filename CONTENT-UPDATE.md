## Technical editorial corrections

Source list is also linked on relevant public technical pages.

- 304/316 austenitic stainless grades are distinguished from precipitation-hardening 17-4PH. Heat treatment is material/condition-specific. https://bssa.org.uk/bssa_articles/general-principles-for-selection-of-stainless-steels/
- Hydrogen-relief baking reduces risk, not a guarantee of elimination; coating and pretreatment are considered together. https://blueprint.fastenal.com/embrittlement.html
- High-strength/hardness risk and specification-based processing replace a universal electroplating ban or bake rule. https://www.wuerth-industrie.com/web/en/wuerthindustrie/technikportal/technische_informationen___tools/oberflaechen/wasserstoffversproedung/wasserstoffversproedung.php
- Shaft fits such as g6/h7 differ from metric thread tolerance classes such as 6g/6H. Grinding is selected according to the actual dimensional/surface requirements, not mandated solely by a fit label. https://www.iso.org/standard/86908.html
- No universal process strength ranking, M24 forging cutoff, flat-die rolling size range or mandatory 100% inspection rule is published.
- Lifting ratings, pressure/leak performance, thread self-locking, chip control and tamper resistance are not inferred from geometry alone.
