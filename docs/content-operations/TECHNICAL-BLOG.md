Write enough to resolve the question without padding or keyword-density targets.

Return READY_FOR_REVIEW, HOLD or NO_CHANGE, plus compared page IDs/hashes, gap evidence, primary intent, draft, source verification, uncertainty, proposed ledger update and suggested publication destination. Apply WRITING-STYLE.md. Resolve the specific reader decision in natural, precise engineering language, with meaningful assumptions and evidence.
